## _The Virgin's Guide to RAGBRAI_

First and foremost, congratulations on your wise decision to ride with the Perineum Falcons. You will probably not regret it.

The following information has been compiled to help guide you through your virgin RAGBRAI experience with the Perineum Falcons.

### Packing for RAGBRAI

Packing for your first RAGBRAI can be a daunting task. Fortunately, the top brass of the Perineum Falcons have taken time out of their extraordinarily busy schedules to provide you with this vigorously researched and exhaustively tested guide to help escort you through the packing process for your virgin RAGBRAI voyage.

#### What you should bring

**Personal Gear**

- **Money**: Figure on about $25-$30 per day on breakfast burritos, pork chops, corn, pie and booze at the local bars. Also, consider pitching in a few dollars on supplemental team beer, Gatorade, Red Bull or whatever else comes up. Lastly, do not forget $300 in personal burial money.
- **Street Clothes**: Barely necessary. Pack light: 2 or 3 outfits, unless you're a female. Then, just try to keep it to a dozen or less, and don't forget your 3 in. pumps. The weather will be mostly hot as balls, but prepare for cool evenings.
- **Shoes/Sandals**
- **Toiletries**
  - Soap
  - Shampoo
  - Deodorant
  - Toothbrush/Toothpaste
  - Feminine Hygiene Products, if you are feminine.
  - Anti-Monkey Butt Powder or some equivocal swamp-ass remedy: Your undercarriage will thank you, as will your shorts. Each day you will spend 6-8 hours with your shorts, and your shorts will be spending 6-8 hours with your sweaty undercarriage. I think you see where I'm going with this. Apply the powder to your inner thighs, the crease where your leg meets your hip, and your taint area. If you are a girl, apply it to your under-boob area. Do it. You will not regret it. If you are a man-boob guy, apply it there as well. Do not be ashamed.
  - Sunscreen
  - Gold Bond / Body Glide / Chamois Butt'r
  - Ibuprofen or equivalent NSAID: There will be aches, pains and headaches
  - Imodium A-D: Stopping during RAGBRAI is not limited to your bike or the bar
  - Blister care (Moleskin, Band-aids, etc.)
  - Wet wipes or flushable wipes: You will thank me later
  - Hand sanitizer
  - Lip balm with SPF
  - Allergy medicine, if applicable

**Cycling Gear**

- **Bike**: Preferably one that fits you and can make it 500 miles in a week. There is no such thing as a stupid bike on RAGBRAI, and you will see everything from $10,000 carbon fiber race machines to Huffy's from Walmart. Though, for the love of god, do not ride a beach cruiser.
- **Helmet**: Technically optional per the ride, but strongly encouraged. You'll be sharing the road with 10,000+ of your closest friends and many of them are idiots.
- **Cycling shorts**: At least 2 pairs. You can rinse one pair out in a sink while wearing the other. Chamois cream is not a suggestion, it is a requirement.
- **Cycling jersey or moisture-wicking shirt**: You will sweat through everything you own. Cotton is rotten.
- **Gloves**: Your hands will thank you.
- **Sunglasses**: For the sun, the wind, and the dust. And for hiding your eyes from the things you will see.
- **Water bottles / hydration pack**: You will drink an obscene amount of water. Plan for it. Gatorade and electrolyte tablets/tabs are also recommended.
- **Bike lights**: Front and rear. You may be riding at dusk or dawn, and you want to be seen.
- **Bike lock**: You will want to leave your bike unattended at bars and food stops. A cheap cable lock is better than nothing.
- **Patch kit / spare tube / pump**: Flat tires are not a matter of if, but when. Be prepared.
- **Small bag or handlebar bag**: For carrying snacks, phone, wallet, and the day's essentials.

**Camping Gear**

- **Tent**: Lightweight is better. You will be setting it up and tearing it down every day.
- **Sleeping bag or blanket**: It can get surprisingly cold at night in Iowa.
- **Sleeping pad or air mattress**: The ground is hard. Your back will thank you.
- **Pillow**: Or a rolled-up sweatshirt. Your call.
- **Camp chair**: The single most important non-cycling item you will bring. You will spend hours sitting in it. Do not skimp.
- **Headlamp or flashlight**: For navigating the tent city at night, and for finding the porta-potties in the dark.

**Miscellaneous**

- **Ziploc bags of various sizes**: For protecting your phone, wallet, and anything else from sweat, rain, and beer.
- **Nalgene or similar wide-mouth water bottle**: For water, and for mixing Gatorade, and for peeing in at 4 AM when you don't want to walk to the porta-potties.
- **Towel**: Don't forget your towel.
- **Costume / Theme outfit**: The Falcons have theme days. You will be judged (favorably) for participation.
- **Sharpie**: For the bus, for your teammates, for the many things you will want to write on.
- **Camera or phone with camera**: You will see things you will not believe and will want to document them for blackmail purposes.
- **Portable charger / battery pack**: You will drain your phone battery taking photos and looking at maps.
- **Earplugs**: For snoring tent neighbors. There will be many.
- **Flip-flops or shower shoes**: For the community showers. Trust me.

#### Tips from the veterans

- The first day is the hardest. It gets easier. Or maybe it doesn't. But you'll be drunk enough not to care.
- Hydrate before you're thirsty. Eat before you're hungry. Apply sunscreen before you're burning.
- The porta-potties are at their cleanest at 6 AM. Plan accordingly.
- If someone offers you a free beer, you take it. This is not a question.
- What happens on RAGBRAI stays on RAGBRAI. Except for the photos. Those go on Facebook.
- Walking tacos are not a myth. They are a lifestyle.
- You will lose something. Accept this now. It is the RAGBRAI tax.
- If you think you've packed enough underwear, pack three more pairs.
